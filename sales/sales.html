What it covers

Everything a school does between the first booking and closing the till.

Board

Sessions on an hourly timeline

Drag a session to another hour or lane. Drag a name onto it to give them a place. Overlapping sessions get their own lanes, so nothing is hidden.

Tide

Plan around the tide, not the clock

Pin a session to "2h before high tide". The tide curve is drawn across the day's board.

Bookings

Courses, packages, deposits

A three-lesson course is three places, and the booking shows how many are still owed. Tiered prices, discounts, named participants with level and wetsuit size.

Online

Your own booking page

Customers choose a product, pick a tier and name who is coming. Bookings land straight in the manager.

Gear

Every board and suit by name

What is out, until when, and when it was last serviced.

Crew

Instructor pay, worked out

What each instructor is owed for the period, from the sessions they taught and their pay rate. Partner hotel and agency commissions too.

Till

Open the till, close it against a count

Takings by payment method and by activity. Each payment gets a numbered receipt, and the receipts are linked so any later change is flagged.

Trips

Boat and van manifests

Departure time, skipper, seats, and a warning for anyone with no signed waiver.

Wifi drops

Keeps working offline

Every change is saved on the device first and syncs when the connection comes back. The till keeps working when the beach wifi doesn't.
