# BRIEF — SHOKOGI home ("A Day in Playa Venao")

Source: interviewed on the three biggest forks (boldness, structure, the one
moment) via the client. Remaining topics authored from established project
context (the SHOKOGI surf-school brand, its live copy, palette and offerings)
and clearly labelled as authored decisions. Not a blind self-author.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (authored):** warm, cinematic, Pacific. Tropical but premium — not a
   loud party, a good day in the water. References: the light of a Panama dawn
   patrol; a surf film's golden-hour closing shot; a well-made travel magazine.

2. **Scroll journey (authored, from the live site's content):**
   dawn hero → surf report → the four ways to surf → what surfers say →
   a quiet glide → sunset invitation. Section by section below.

3. **Energy curve (authored):** calm open, grounded, a bright midday high on the
   offerings, warm afternoon, a deliberate quiet dip, then a deep (not loud)
   sunset close.

4. **Feeling, stage by stage + the one moment (client, elaborated):**
   The one moment = **"a day at the beach"** — the whole page moves through a
   single day as you scroll: the sky, the sun and the light go from dawn to
   sunset. Stage feelings in the curve below.

5. **One thing no other site does (client → signature):** the page *is* a day.
   A continuous day-cycle sky (dawn→sunrise→midday→golden→sunset) driven by
   global scroll, with the sun travelling its own arc as the only progress
   indicator. Coded in the page, not an engine parameter.

6. **Range (client):** cinematic & rich. Layered depth, restrained motion,
   premium — not minimal, not experimental-brutalist.

7. **One world or distinct scenes (client):** **distinct scenes / chapters.**
   Each section is its own scene with its own device and layout. The day-cycle
   sky is the single continuous thread that unifies the chapters.

8. **Assets (authored, from environment):** no photoreal generation here
   (no kie.ai key, no ffmpeg) and the live Shopify media sits behind a blocked
   CDN. So the demo world is built in CSS/SVG (sky, sea, sun, headland, palms).
   The LIVE build on Shopify swaps in the real hero video and programme photos.

## The feeling curve (one line per act)

1. Dawn / Hero — stillness and promise. Empty lineup, offshore, first light.
2. Morning / Report — grounded trust. Four plain facts about the place.
3. Midday / The four ways — possibility, brightness. The choice, wide open.
4. Afternoon / Voices — warmth, belonging. Real surfers, golden light.
5. Golden / Glide — awe, a held breath. One quiet line. (the dip before the peak)
6. Sunset / Invitation — belonging and welcome. The day resolves. **PEAK.**

## The peak

The sunset close. The sky reaches full sunset exactly as the page asks you to
come surf. The sentence a visitor says to a friend:
**"You scroll and the whole page goes through a day — it ends at sunset right
when it asks you to come."**
Lives in Act 6, the largest span, the quiet Act 5 set deliberately before it.

## Tell-someone sentence

**It's the site where scrolling moves the sun across a whole day in Playa Venao,
and lands you at sunset on the invitation to surf.**

## Authored silence

Act 5 (Golden / Glide) is intentionally quiet — one line, lots of sky, slow
light. It is the held breath before the sunset peak, not dead scroll.
