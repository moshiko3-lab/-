# SHOKOGI — scroll-craft brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The owner chose "a full,
separate scroll-craft page" and, across this project, set firm brand direction:
white + Shokogi pink (#f46e95) + a touch of near-black, clean / modern /
premium / refined (not gaudy, not childish), Barlow Condensed + Figtree. Those
are honored as hard rules; the staging below is authored to them.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe (3–5 words) + references.** Clean, premium, oceanic, warm, refined.
   References (medium, not sites): the quiet of first light on an empty beach;
   an editorial surf magazine spread; the feeling of a wave sheet washing sand
   smooth.
2. **Scroll journey (their sequence).** Arrive at the water → the promise
   (waves, all levels, all year) → the ways to surf → what surfers say → the
   foil moment (glide) → come surf with us.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm arrival → steady confidence → light, playful middle →
   warm → one lift → settled welcome. Never loud the whole way.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage + the one moment.** See feeling curve below. The
   one moment: the foil "glide above the waves" lift.
5. **One thing no site they've seen does.** A living shoreline — a sheet of
   water (the "tide") that follows the pointer along the hero and, at the peak,
   washes up the page to uncover the headline, like a wave cleaning the sand.
6. **How far from premium-minimal.** Premium-minimal, warm. Refined, airy,
   confident type. No drama for drama's sake.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct scenes / chapters, each behaving
   differently. Not one continuous flight.
8. **Assets they have.** Real hero + foil footage and surf photography exist on
   their store, but the Shopify CDN is unreachable from this environment and no
   image-generation key is available, so this build's world is crafted in CSS
   and SVG in the brand palette. Honest limitation, stated in the report.

## Feeling curve (emotion — what on screen causes it)

1. Arrival — *calm anticipation.* A pale dawn horizon; the tide breathes at the
   foot of the headline.
2. Promise — *quiet confidence.* Three plain truths on open white space.
3. Ways — *easy curiosity.* Four warm panels slide past, pick-your-wave.
4. Voices — *belonging / warmth.* Real words from real surfers, unhurried.
5. Glide — **exhilaration (THE PEAK).** The water washes up and the headline
   lifts: "Glide above the waves." Largest span; the act before it is quiet.
6. Join — *settled welcome.* One near-black room, one pink action.

## The peak

"The part where the wave washes up the screen and the words rise out of it."
Lives in Act 5 (Glide). It gets the most scroll room and the quiet of Act 4
before it.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **the tide follows your cursor and, at the end, a wave
washes up the screen to lift the words out of the water.**

## Authored silence

Act 4 (Voices) is deliberately quiet and slow — the held breath before the
peak. Not dead scroll.
