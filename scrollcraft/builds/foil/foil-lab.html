What is Foil Surfing?

You've tried foiling at home. Now experience it in paradise.

Playa Venao offers what most foil spots can't: three distinct locations within 5 minutes, each offering different conditions and progression opportunities. Our protected bay provides consistent, clean swells perfect for building confidence and duration.

At Shokogi, we provide what serious foilers need to progress: expert jetski tow-in support that lets you maximize water time, high-performance equipment dialed for these conditions, and local knowledge of where to foil based on swell, wind, and tide.

the advantage

Why Playa Venao for Foil Surfing?

Most foil spots lock you into one break with variable conditions. Playa Venao offers multiple locations within minutes—we choose the best spot daily based on swell, wind, and your ability.

Expert Tow-In Support: Our jetski assistance eliminates exhausting paddle-outs and positioning guesswork. You maximize attempts and water time. Most visiting foilers are shocked at how much more riding they get compared to self-paddling.

World-Class Conditions: Year-round 78-82°F water (no thick wetsuits), consistent Pacific swells (never flat), and uncrowded lineups (often entire sections to yourself).

Your Progression Path

1

First Session

Your first session focuses on adapting to new equipment and conditions. We'll assess your current ability, dial in board/foil setup for your style, and get you comfortable with our tow-in system.

Building Consistency

Ready to push your riding? Longer sessions let you work on advanced techniques: connecting multiple swells, riding tiny bumps, speed pumping, and positioning for bigger conditions.

FAQ

What level of foiling experience do I need?
You should have gotten up on a foil before, even if just for a few rides during 1-2 lessons. We focus on progression and technique—not teaching people to foil for the first time.
I've only foiled once or twice—is that enough experience?
Yes! If you've gotten up and ridden a foil, you have the foundation we need. Our tow-in support and expert coaching will help you build from there.
Do you teach complete beginners?
Not currently. We're focusing on progression for riders who've already tried foiling. This allows us to maximize safety and provide the best experience for visiting foilers.
What makes your tow-in support different?
Our instructors are experienced watermen and skilled foilers themselves—not just boat drivers. The tow-in eliminates exhausting paddle-outs and missed waves, so you spend maximum time actually riding.
