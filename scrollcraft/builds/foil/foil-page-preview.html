What is Foil Surfing?

You've tried foiling at home. Now experience it in paradise. Playa Venao offers three distinct locations within 5 minutes, each offering different conditions and progression opportunities. Our protected bay provides consistent, clean swells perfect for building confidence and duration.

step by step

Your Progression Path

1

First Session

We assess your current ability, dial in board/foil setup for your style, and get you comfortable with our tow-in system. The consistent, clean energy lets you focus on technique without fighting chop or closeouts.

2

Building Consistency

Sessions focus on specific goals: longer rides, pumping efficiency, wave selection, or speed control. With expert tow-in support, you maximize attempts—no exhausting paddle-outs or missed waves.

3

Push Limits

Longer sessions let you work on advanced techniques: connecting multiple swells, riding tiny bumps, speed pumping, and positioning for bigger conditions.

FAQ

What level of foiling experience do I need?
You should have gotten up on a foil before, even if just for a few rides during 1-2 lessons. We focus on progression and technique—not teaching people to foil for the first time.
What makes your tow-in support different?
Our instructors are experienced watermen and skilled foilers themselves—not just boat drivers. The tow-in eliminates exhausting paddle-outs and missed waves, so you spend maximum time actually riding.
What's the best time of year to visit?
Year-round! Playa Venao receives consistent Pacific swells in every season, with warm water (78-82°F) year-round.
