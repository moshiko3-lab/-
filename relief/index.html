What happened

A 7.7 earthquake, a few kilometres from our beach

On Friday 9 October 2026 a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck southern Panama, followed by aftershocks of up to 6.6. The worst damage was in Veraguas, Herrera and Los Santos, the province Playa Venao is in.

7.7Magnitude (USGS), at a shallow depth of about 12.6 km
1882The last time Panama felt an earthquake this strong
350+Aftershocks recorded in the first three days (Panama's Geosciences Institute)
6.6Strongest aftershocks, felt the same day

In Playa Venao, multi-storey hotel and hostel buildings collapsed or were badly damaged. Panama's tourism authority (ATP) lists Venao establishments among those awaiting structural evaluation.

In Pedasí district, families spent the night in parks and sports centres, afraid to go back inside. The bridge over the Oria River was closed for structural damage, and neighbouring districts went without water and power.

Many of the places to stay along our beach were destroyed or badly damaged, and the beach itself is being restored. It will take time before travellers come back to Venao, and a surf school lives from those visitors.

Shokogi itself was not damaged: the school and the boards are here and we are ready to teach. What the earthquake took from us is the people who come to surf. Until they return, every lesson you buy ahead and every lycra you wear keeps the school going.

FAQ

Questions

Who receives the money?

Shokogi Surf School, Playa Venao, which runs this campaign. Every contribution goes to keeping the school going until visitors return.

When can I use my lesson?

Any time you come to Venao, starting now. Lessons stay valid for 24 months from purchase. Message us to book a time.

Can I give the lesson as a gift?

Yes. Tell us the name of the person who will surf and we will put the lesson under their name.

I can't donate. How else can I help?

Share this page, and when you plan a trip, come to Venao. Every visitor helps the beach come back.
