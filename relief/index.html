    FAQ

    Questions

    What is the beach like right now?

    Venao is getting back to normal, step by step. The supermarket and the gas station are already open, many businesses on the beach have reopened, and from early to mid-November there will be places to stay again.

    Who receives the money?

    Shokogi Surf School, Playa Venao, which runs this campaign. Every purchase helps keep the school going through this season, and with your help we'll set up a surf program for local kids from the villages around Playa Venao.

    How do I get my order?

    We ship it to you; shipped orders can take up to 90 days to arrive. You can also collect it at the school in Playa Venao. Enter your address at checkout to see the shipping cost.

    Are you open?

    Yes. Shokogi is back and ready to welcome surfers, with our regular schedule from early November.

    I can't buy right now. How else can I help?

    Share this page, and when you plan a trip, come to Venao. Every visitor helps the beach come back.
