After the 7.7 earthquake · 9 October 2026
Wear Shokogi. Help us get back in the water.

On 9 October a 7.7 earthquake hit Panama's Azuero coast, a few kilometres from our beach. The good news: Venao is getting back to normal, step by step. The supermarket and the gas station are open, many businesses on the beach have reopened, there will be places to stay again from early to mid-November, and the waves are as good as ever.

We expect to be back in the water by mid-November.
