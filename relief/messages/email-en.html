After the 7.7 earthquake · 9 October 2026
Venao is still here.

On 9 October a 7.7 earthquake hit Panama's Azuero coast, a few kilometres from our beach. It hit all of Venao, us included. The good news: the waves are as good as ever, and Venao is recovering.

We hope and expect to soft-open in early November, with the full opening in early to mid December.

Until then, you can help Shokogi through this season, and surf it when you come:
